## Translation starts before the translator

Content becomes easier to translate when its structure is explicit. A stable
slug, shared image intent, and visible review status give every language the
same editorial foundation without forcing every sentence to look identical.

Keeping localized files beside one another also changes the review habit.
Missing work is obvious, differences are intentional, and a contributor can
improve one language without opening application code.

## Preserve meaning, not formatting

A translation system should protect meaning and navigation while leaving room
for each language to sound natural. Shared schemas enforce the contract; human
review protects the voice.
