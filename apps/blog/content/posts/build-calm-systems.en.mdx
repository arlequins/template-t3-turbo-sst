## Make the next decision visible

Good systems do not ask people to remember everything. They make ownership,
status, and the next useful decision visible at the moment it matters.

We start with three questions:

- What decision is blocked?
- Who can move it forward?
- What evidence will make the decision easier?

The result is not more process. It is less time spent reconstructing context
and more room for careful work.

## Prefer rhythm over urgency

A weekly review, a small decision log, and explicit review states are usually
more durable than a stream of urgent messages. Calm is not the absence of
ambition. It is the infrastructure that lets ambition last.
